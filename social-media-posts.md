# Social Media Content: Revalidation Readiness Quiz

## Strategy Overview
The goal is to drive traffic to the new `/quiz` page by triggering "Revalidation Anxiety" and offering immediate relief. We avoid "selling" RevalPro directly; instead, we sell the *result* of the quiz (peace of mind).

---

## 1. Facebook Group Post (The "Help Me" Angle)
**Target:** Nursing groups, Student Nurse groups.
**Tone:** Humble, asking for feedback (avoids "self-promotion" bans).

**Headline:** "Is this accurate? Building a free tool for revalidation anxiety."

**Body:**
Hi everyone,

I've been working on a free tool to help nurses quickly check if they're actually ready for revalidation (or if they're missing something critical).

It's a 60-second "Readiness Quiz" that gives you a score and a checklist of what's missing. No login required to take it.

I'd love some feedback from the community:
1. Does the score feel accurate to where you are?
2. Is the checklist helpful?

Here is the link: [Link to Quiz]

Thanks in advance! P.S. It's completely free, just trying to make something useful.

## 2. Instagram/Facebook Story (The "Fear" Angle)
**Visual:** A simple text graphic with a red background or a "stressed nurse" photo.

**Text Overlay:**
"Do you know if you have enough practice hours logged?"
"Don't wait until the last month."

**Link Sticker:** "Check Readiness Score" -> [Link to Quiz]

**Caption:**
The #1 mistake we see? Nurses thinking they have "plenty of time" until they realize they're missing a confirmation meeting. Take the 60-second check to be sure. 🩺✅ #revalidation #nursinguk #nmc

## 3. LinkedIn Post (The "Professional" Angle)

- Confirmation status

If you're a Practice Educator or Manager, feel free to share this with your team to help them stay on track.
